Hungry

fault adjective (NEEDING FOOD)

wanting or needing food:
example:

  • By four o'clock I was really hungry.
  • feel hungry I was beginning to feel very hungry.
  • The children are always hungry when they get home from school.
  • There are too many hungry people (= people without enough to eat) in the world.
  • go hungry She often goes hungry herself (= does not eat) so that her children can have enough to eat.
  • hungry work Digging the garden is hungry work (= makes you feel hunger).
